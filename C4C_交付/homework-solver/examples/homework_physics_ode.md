# PHYSICS 201 — University Physics, Homework 4
## 力学 · 电磁学 · 热学

### Problem 1
A net force of 12 N acts on a 3.0 kg mass that is initially at rest.
Find the acceleration of the mass and the net force needed to keep it moving
at constant velocity.

### Problem 2
A ball is dropped from rest in vacuum. How fast is it moving after 3.0 s,
and how far has it fallen? Take $g = 9.8$ m/s^2.

### Problem 3
Find the kinetic energy of a 2.5 kg cart rolling at 6.0 m/s.

### Problem 4
A 4.0 kg object is lifted 3.0 m by a vertical force. Find the change in
its gravitational potential energy.

### Problem 5
Two point charges of $3.0$ C and $5.0$ C are placed 2 m apart in vacuum.
Find the magnitude of the Coulomb force between them.

### Problem 6
A potential difference of 24 V is applied across a 6 ohm resistor.
Find the current through it and the power dissipated.

### Problem 7
What electric field strength corresponds to a force of 8.0 N acting on a
charge of 4.0 C?

### Problem 8
A gas sample occupies 0.05 m^3 at a temperature of 350 K and a pressure of
202650 Pa. Find the number of moles present.

## Problem Set B: Differential Equations

### Problem 9
Solve the initial value problem $y' + 3y = 6$ subject to $y(0) = 2$.

### Problem 10
Find the general solution of $y' - 5y = 0$.

### Problem 11
Solve the second-order homogeneous equation $y'' - 7y' + 12y = 0$.

### Problem 12
Solve the separable differential equation $\frac{dy}{dx} = x y$.

## Problem Set C: Mixed

### Problem 13
A population model obeys $y' = 0.2y$ with $y(0) = 100$. Find $y(t)$ and
interpret the growth constant.

### Problem 14
Verify that $y = C_1 e^{2x} + C_2 e^{3x}$ solves $y'' - 5y' + 6y = 0$,
and state whether every solution has this form.
