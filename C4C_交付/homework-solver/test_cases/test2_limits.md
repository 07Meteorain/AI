# Math 1A Worksheet 4: Limits of Functions

## Questions

1. Suppose $f$ is a function and $L$ and $a$ are real numbers.

(a) Describe what is meant by $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ as best you can.
(b) Graph a function $y = f(x)$ such that $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$, and $f(a) = L$.
(c) Graph a function $y = f(x)$ such that $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$, but $f(a) \neq L$.
(d) Graph a function $y = f(x)$ such that $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$, and $f(a)$ does not exist.

2. Is $\infty$ a number? What does $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = \infty$ mean? Does $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = \infty$ mean that the limit exists?

3. What does the Squeeze Theorem say? Draw a picture illustrating this theorem.

4. What do we mean by $\lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) = L$? What is meant by $\lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = L$? Graph a function $y = f(x)$ and some point $x = a$ where $\lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) \neq \lim_{x \to a^+} f(x)$. Find an algebraic expression for such a function.

## Problems

1. (a) Is the limit of a sum always the sum of the limits? Give an example where $\lim_{x \to a}[f(x) + g(x)]$ exists even though neither $\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$ nor $\lim_{x \to a} g(x)$ exist.

(b) Is the limit of a product always the product of the limits? Give an example where $\lim_{x \to a}[f(x)g(x)]$ exists even though neither $\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$ nor $\lim_{x \to a} g(x)$ exist.

2. (a) Find $\lim_{x \to 0^+}(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{|x|})$, $\lim_{x \to 0^-}(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{|x|})$, and $\lim_{x \to 0}(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{|x|})$, if they exist.

(b) Show that $\lim_{x \to 0} |x| = 0$.

(c) Show that $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{|x|}{x}$ does not exist.

3. For what kinds of functions can one evaluate the limit $\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$ by just plugging in $x = a$? Give an example of a function $f(x)$ for which the limit $\lim_{x \to a} f(x)$ exists and can be evaluated, yet for which this substitution doesn't work.

## Additional Problems

1. Below are listed three limits. Match the best method to each limit and evaluate each limit.

(a) $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^{1000} - 703x^{506} + \pi x^{17} + 480}{-372x^{75} + 39x^{14} + \sqrt{7}x^5 - 24}$

(b) $\lim_{x \to 4} \frac{x^2 + 2x - 24}{x - 4}$

(c) $\lim_{x \to 0} x^2 \sin(\frac{1}{x})$

2. Using a calculator, try to guess $\lim_{n \to \infty} n \sin(\frac{\pi}{n})$. (Remember that $n$ is in radians, not degrees!)

3. Draw a circle of radius 1. Inscribe a regular $n$-gon inside it.

(a) What is the measure of the angles formed around the center of the circle?
(b) What is the area of one triangle? What is the area of the polygon?
(c) Calculate $\lim_{n \to \infty}$ (Area of $n$-gon). Does this answer make sense intuitively?
